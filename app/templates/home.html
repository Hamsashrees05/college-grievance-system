How it works

  1. Submit. Choose a category, describe the problem and attach evidence if you have any.
  2. Assigned. The right department officer receives it automatically.
  3. Tracked. You see every status change and reply on your dashboard and get notified.
  4. Escalated if late. Missed deadlines go to the head of department, then the grievance committee.
  5. Confirm. When it is resolved you rate the outcome, or reopen it if the problem remains.

Your privacy

You can file a complaint anonymously. Officers and heads of department then never see your name. Only the grievance committee and the administrator can, and every time they look it is recorded in the audit log.

Complaints about harassment or ragging go straight to the grievance committee and are hidden from ordinary officers.
