Privacy and data protection

What we collect

Your name, college e-mail, department, role and optional phone number when you register, and the text and files you add to a complaint. We collect nothing else and use no tracking or advertising cookies. The only cookie is the login session cookie.

Why we collect it

Only to receive, route, resolve and report on grievances, and to notify you about your own complaints.

Who can see your complaint

You, the officer or head of department it is assigned to, the grievance committee for escalated or sensitive cases, and the administrator. Internal staff notes are never shown to you.

Anonymous complaints

If you choose “Submit anonymously”, your identity is hidden from officers and heads of department. The grievance committee and the administrator can still see it so that serious cases can be investigated, and each such view is written to the audit log. Anonymity cannot be guaranteed if the content of a complaint identifies you.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Uploaded files are checked, renamed and served only to people who may see the complaint. Every important action is recorded in an audit log.

Keeping and deleting data

Complaint records are kept for the period set by the college policy so that trends and decisions can be reviewed. Ask the administrator to correct or delete your account data.

False complaints

Complaints must be truthful. Knowingly false or abusive complaints can lead to disciplinary action under college rules.
