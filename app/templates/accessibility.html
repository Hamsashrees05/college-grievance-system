Accessibility statement

This site is built to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader and on small screens.

  • Every form field has a visible label and errors are announced as alerts.
  • Status is always shown as text, never by colour alone.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is the first item on every page, and focus is clearly outlined.
  • Layouts adapt from phone to desktop, and animations are disabled when your system asks for reduced motion.

If something is hard to use, tell the administrator and we will fix it.
