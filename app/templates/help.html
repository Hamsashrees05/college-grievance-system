Help and about

Who can use the system

Students and staff submit and track complaints. Department officers resolve them. Heads of department handle escalations. The grievance committee handles serious or sensitive cases. The administrator manages users, categories and reports.

Status meanings

Submitted
Received and waiting for review.
Under Review / In Progress
Someone is looking at it or working on it.
Awaiting Info
The officer needs more details from you. The deadline clock pauses until you reply.
Resolved
The officer says it is fixed. Please confirm and rate, or reopen within 7 days if it is not.
Closed
You confirmed the outcome.
Rejected
It cannot be handled, and the reason is given.
Reopened
You told us the problem was not solved, so it is being reviewed again.

Files you can attach

PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 5 MB each, and up to 5 files per complaint.
